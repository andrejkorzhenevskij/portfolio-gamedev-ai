# Rules

You are putting together a robbery plan in five parts. In each part, cards go in order from 1 to 5.

You cannot see your own cards, but you can see your partner's. On your turn, play a card, toss one into the discard pile, or tell your partner a color or rank.

A correct card advances the plan. A wrong one burns the rest of that lane and costs you a mistake. The third mistake ends the job on the spot.

A hint spends one token and marks every matching card. Discarding restores a token. Completing a lane restores one too.

An ordinary hint earns the giver a cut. Advice starts at 10% and gets 10% dearer every time you take it. Refusing to advise costs nothing.

Every played card is worth one point. 25 is the lot. 16 is enough for a good way out.

The deck holds 50 cards in five colors. Each color has three 1s, two 2s, two 3s, two 4s, and one 5.

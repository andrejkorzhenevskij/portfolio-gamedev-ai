I was the best west of the East Coast.

### picture

They'd put a handsome price on my head in eight states.

I was damn dangerous. Trouble was, it was damn dangerous being me.

Then the railroad reached Texas. Tomorrow, the last payroll stagecoach rolls through here. They'll give it a proper escort in town, which means our last chance to take it comes just before.

I meant to use that chance: make one good score, then disappear with the money.

I reached this border town at sundown.

I was late.

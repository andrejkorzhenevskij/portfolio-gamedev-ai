# What is what on the table

## `partner_hand` — partner's hand

Your partner's cards face you. You know their colors and ranks; he does not. You may tell him a color or a rank, marking every matching card at once. That takes a turn.

## `player_hand` — your hand

You hold your own cards facing away. Your partner sees them; you do not. And yes, he can tell you a color or a rank. If he feels like it.

## `plan_lanes` — the plan

Five lanes: Guns, The Lead, Escort, The Mark, and Getaway. Cards go down in order from 1 to 5. A cross means somebody played crooked and the rest of that part of the plan is gone.

## `mistakes` — glasses and bottle

The first mistake empties one glass, the second empties the other. The third tips the bottle and ends the job.

## `draw_deck` — the deck

The number on the deck tells you how many cards remain. There are 50 in all: five colors, each with three 1s, two 2s, two 3s, two 4s, and one 5.

## `hint_tokens` — hint tokens

These pay for ordinary hints. Every hint spends one token. Discarding a card gives one back.

## `share_note` — the cut

Shows how the take is split right now. Once the plan is good enough, this is also where you can stop tempting fate and leave with what you have. Advice shifts the cut toward the man doing the helping.

## `ask_advice` — ask for advice

When things look bad, your partner will point to a playable card if he sees one. It costs a hint token and a piece of the take, and the price keeps climbing. The number on the button is the next price.

## `discard_pile` — discard pile

Every discarded card and every bad play ends up here. Click any card for a closer look. Study the pile; it is one way to work out what is hiding in your own hand.

## `status_line` — status line

Tells you, briefly, what just happened at the table.

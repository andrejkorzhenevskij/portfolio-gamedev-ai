I'd rather have run into Wyatt Earp. Honest.

### picture

I knew him. He was good. As good as me, except for the bit where he wasn't.

No need to ask what brought him here. The marked-up map in his hands settled that.

We froze. What were we supposed to do, shoot? The survivor would be in irons before the smoke cleared. Walk away? He'd sell me out by nightfall.

Well. I would.

I didn't trust him a nickel's worth, but there wasn't another play.

We had one night to make a plan together.

### fade

The stagecoach came through at dawn. My last job.
